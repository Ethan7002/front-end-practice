HEALTH & SCIENCE

The quiet power of sleep

For many people, sleep is the first thing they when life becomes busy. Yet scientists increasingly describe sleep as an biological process rather than a passive break from the day.

During deep sleep, the brain does not simply switch off. It memories, sorts new information and removes waste that has accumulated during waking hours. This nightly maintenance helps us think clearly and respond to difficult situations with greater .

“Sleep is not time lost. It is preparation for everything that follows.”

A consistent routine can be more than trying to catch up at the weekend. Small habits—such as dimming lights, avoiding late caffeine and putting phones away—send the body a reliable signal that it is time to rest.

The goal is not perfect sleep every night. It is to create the in which good sleep becomes more likely, and to treat rest as part of a healthy life rather than a reward earned after exhaustion.
